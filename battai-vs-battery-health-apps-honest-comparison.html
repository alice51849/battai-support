BattAI vs Battery Life vs BAST vs Battery Testing: an honest look at iPhone battery health apps

Last checked: November 26, 2026. App Store details change; tap through to each listing for the current version and price.

We make BattAI. This page compares it with the top three results for “battery health check” in US App Store search (checked November 26, 2026), using only what each app’s own App Store listing says. Where another app does something better, we say so.

Quick answer

  • Want the app to read maximum capacity and cycle count for you? Battery Life or BAST may suit you better; both say they read these values from Apple’s analytics files. Want AirPods and other accessories too? Battery Life covers those. BattAI can’t read maximum capacity by itself.
  • Mostly want runtime estimates? Battery Life and Battery Testing both focus on how long your remaining charge lasts. BattAI gives a single estimate instead: about how many hours until 20% at your recent pace, as a range.
  • Want to know how fast your battery is aging, when it may hit 80%, and a record of every charge? That is what BattAI is built for.

Where the others are stronger

  • Battery Life says it has been downloaded by millions of users since 2014, has tens of thousands of ratings, reads health values from iOS analytics files, and covers AirPods, keyboards, game controllers and other accessories. Its one-time Pro upgrade is listed at a lower price than BattAI’s Lifetime Pro on the US App Store (November 26, 2026), and adds battery health charts, the full history of readings and iCloud sync.
  • BAST is a focused diagnostics tool. It reads cycle count and current, maximum and minimum capacity from Apple’s analytics log files, and its listing shows no in-app purchases.
  • Battery Testing is a long-running runtime tester; its only listed paid option is ad removal.
  • BattAI has no ratings in the US yet, so you have less to go on from other users.

Where BattAI is different

  • A plain answer, not just a number. Great shape, normal range, or below the 80% mark Apple uses for battery service, plus whether it is aging slower than, about the same as, or faster than Apple’s design target for your model. These are estimates, not a factory test.
  • When to replace it. A capacity curve with a line at 80%, and when it may drop to 80% as a season or range, never a made-up date. With too little data, it asks for another reading.
  • Every charge recorded. With a second Shortcuts automation, every plug-in and unplug is logged even with the app closed.
  • Is this charger fast? An estimated speed level and power range, measured between 20% and 80%. Apple doesn’t let apps read charging power, so it’s estimated from how fast the level rises.
  • A battery passport (document or long image) with every reading, its date and source, for when you sell or repair your iPhone.
  • Private. No account, no ads, no servers; your data stays on your iPhone. BattAI doesn’t save battery, cool your phone or change the status bar icon.
