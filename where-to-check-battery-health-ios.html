Where to check iPhone battery health, cycle count and warning messages

Open Settings → Battery → Battery Health to see Maximum Capacity and Peak Performance Capability (on earlier iOS versions the item is called Battery Health & Charging). On iPhone 15 models and later, Settings → General → About also shows the battery cycle count. Below are the path differences between versions, what each number and message means, and what to do when you see a warning.

Battery Health settings path by iOS version

Apple’s current instructions are: open the Settings app, tap Battery, then tap Battery Health. Depending on your iOS version and model, the names can differ slightly:

SituationPath you are likely to see
Current iOS (per Apple Support)Settings > Battery > Battery Health
Earlier iOS, iPhone 14 models and earlierSettings > Battery > Battery Health & Charging
Charging options on iPhone 15 models and laterSettings > Battery > Charging (Charge Limit lives here)
Much older iOS (around iOS 11.3)Settings > Battery > Battery Health (Beta)

If you cannot find it, type “battery health” into the search field at the top of Settings. Battery Health is available on iPhone 6 and later; iPad does not have the same Battery Health screen in Settings.

The two things on the screen

  • Maximum Capacity: your battery’s capacity relative to when it was new. A new phone showing slightly under 100% is normal.
  • Peak Performance Capability: whether the battery can deliver high instantaneous power. “Your battery is currently supporting normal peak performance” means all is well.

Where to see the battery cycle count

Cycle count tells you where your battery is relative to its design life. Apple says iPhone 14 models and earlier are designed to retain 80% of original capacity at 500 complete charge cycles, and iPhone 15 models at 1000, under ideal conditions.

  • iPhone 15 models and later: go to Settings > General > About and look for the battery section to see the cycle count (some models also show manufacture date and first use). Exactly what appears can vary by iOS version and model.
  • iPhone 14 models and earlier: Settings usually does not show the cycle count directly. Apple or an authorized provider can run a diagnostic for you.
How cycles are counted. One cycle is using energy equal to 100% of the battery’s capacity, cumulatively, not one plug-in. Apple also notes that a complete charge cycle is normalized between 80% and 100% of original capacity to account for capacity loss over time.

A one-minute monthly battery check

Batteries age slowly, so checking on a fixed day each month tells you more than checking whenever you remember. Copy this list:

  1. Open Settings > Battery > Battery Health and note Maximum Capacity.
  2. Look at Peak Performance Capability for any new message.
  3. On iPhone 15 models and later, note the cycle count in Settings > General > About.
  4. Compare with last month: how much did capacity drop and how many cycles were added? Dividing the capacity drop by the new cycles gives a rough aging rate you can watch over time.
  5. Check charging settings: Charge Limit on iPhone 15 models and later, or Optimized Battery Charging on iPhone 14 models and earlier.

Frequently asked questions

Can I check battery health on iPad?

iPad Settings does not include the same Battery Health screen as iPhone. If you suspect a battery problem, contact Apple Support for a diagnostic.

Why can’t I find Battery Health in Settings?

Your iOS version may label it Battery Health & Charging, or your model may be too old (the feature is on iPhone 6 and later). Searching “battery” at the top of Settings is the quickest route.

Can I keep using my iPhone if it says “Unable to verify”?

Usually yes, but the battery health information shown may not be accurate. Have it checked by Apple or an authorized provider if you want to know its real condition.

Is “Your battery’s health is significantly degraded” dangerous?

Apple says the message does not indicate a safety issue and you can still use the battery, though battery life and performance problems will be more noticeable. A replacement restores full performance and capacity.

What data does BattAI read?

According to its App Store description, BattAI uses five values from public iOS APIs and, from a file, reads only cycle count, nominal charge capacity and design capacity. Other file data is discarded immediately and never stored or sent.
