How BattAI works

Clear rules, sources, and limits

BattAI uses five values from public iOS APIs: battery level, charge state, Low Power Mode, whole-device thermal state, and time. Other results are calculated from those readings or added by you. Each detail shows its source and limits.

Import battery details

Choose an iOS diagnostic file. BattAI reads supported battery fields and labels their source.

Export data

Create a file on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

Understand your battery over time

Unlock deeper history, planning, and reports.

Privacy

No ads, analytics, tracking, third-party SDKs, or internet requests. Data stays here, except for a minimal snapshot sent directly to your paired Apple Watch.
