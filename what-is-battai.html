What is BattAI?

BattAI is an iPhone and iPad app that works as an ongoing battery health check and battery monitor. Settings shows today's maximum capacity, not how it changes; BattAI records battery level, charging and heat as you use your phone and turns them into a 0–100 battery health score, charging insights, trends and reports.

Who is it for?

People who want to know whether their battery is wearing out and why: someone deciding between a battery replacement and a new phone, someone who wants to charge more gently, or someone about to sell a phone and wanting a one-page battery report for the buyer.

How does pricing work?

As described on the App Store, the battery card (live level and charging state) and the full, low battery and heat alerts can be used without buying anything. The health score, charging insights, trends, reports, widgets and Apple Watch analysis unlock with one purchase. It is not a subscription and there are no automatic charges.

How is it different from other battery apps?

  • It scores health from five named factors (capacity 40%, cycle count 25%, device age 10%, heat exposure 10%, charging habits 15%) and calls out the main one.
  • It charts maximum capacity over time with a 95% confidence range, and scores every charge from 0 to 100.
  • It exports a resale report for a buyer, plus PDF, PNG, CSV and JSON.
  • No account, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs and no network requests. Data stays on the device.

Want to try it on your iPhone? BattAI scores your battery health, charts maximum capacity over time and alerts you when the battery is full or hot.

More questions

Can BattAI read my maximum capacity by itself?

No. You enter the value shown in Settings › Battery, or import it from the system's Analytics Data file. BattAI reads only cycle count, nominal charge capacity and design capacity from that file.

Is the score an official value?

No. Scores and estimates are calculated, not official or manufacturer values, and not a promise of how long your battery will last.

Does it work on iPad and Apple Watch?

Yes. On iPad, level, charging, drain, heat, alerts, reports and widgets work as on iPhone, though the health score needs a value you enter. There is also a Watch app with complications.

BattAI is an independent app and is not affiliated with Apple. Its battery health score is calculated by BattAI's published rules and is not the Maximum Capacity value in iOS Settings.
